How this works

Say the answer to yourself, or scribble it down, before you flip the card. Then be honest with the buttons. Again means you didn't have it, and the card comes back in a few cards' time. Hard, Good and Easy push it further into the future each time you get it, so cards you know well drift out to weeks and months while shaky ones keep turning up. The small text under each button shows when you'll see the card next.

Pick lectures with the chips above (the number on a chip is how many of its cards are due). New cards are capped each day so the reviews don't snowball. On a keyboard, press Space to flip and 1 to 4 to grade.

Each card you remember pays 1 oz of gold once a day, a card that reaches a three-week interval pays 2 oz, and clearing a session of five or more cards pays a 5 oz bonus once a day. Review gold is kept separately from each lecture's score and is added to your total on the lectures page. Your schedule is saved in this browser, and the backup code on the lectures page carries it to your other devices.
